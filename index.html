How to Play

  • Move the mouse to aim – the camera turns around your ball. Look up/down to change your view.
  • Hold the left button and drag back (towards you) to set power. Release to putt. Esc or right-click cancels a swing.
  • Hold Space for a bird’s-eye overview. ←/→ (or A/D) fine-tune your aim.
  • Water, lava and falling off the course cost one penalty stroke and your ball returns to where you last putted.
  • Each hole is capped at 10 strokes. Beat par to celebrate!
  • R restarts the hole · M mutes · F replays the fly-over.
  • Choose a Hole on the main menu (or Jump to Hole when paused) starts practice play – then [ / ] hop between holes.
